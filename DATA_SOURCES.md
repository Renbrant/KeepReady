# KeepReady — Authoritative Public Data Sources & Scientific Standards

This document catalogs all verified scientific databases, government APIs, humanitarian nutrition benchmarks, civil defense frameworks, empirical food preservation standards, and free public-domain survival literature integrated into **KeepReady**.

---

## 1. Food Safety, Shelf-Life & Long-Term Preservation Research

* **[USDA FSIS FoodKeeper Dataset](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/fsis-foodkeeper-data)**
  - *Publishing Body:* USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service, Cornell University, and FMI.
  - *Format:* JSON / CSV open dataset on Data.gov (Public Domain).
  - *Application:* Provides empirical shelf-life baselines across ambient room-temperature pantry, cool cellar, refrigeration, and freezer storage for over 500 food categories.

* **[USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning (Agriculture Information Bulletin No. 539)](https://nchfp.uga.edu/resources/category/usda-guide)**
  - *Publishing Body:* United States Department of Agriculture (USDA / NCHFP).
  - *Format:* Open-access public domain technical bulletin.
  - *Application:* The global gold standard for home preservation: precise pressure canning time/temperature tables for low-acid foods (meat, vegetables, soups) and elevation-specific boiling time adjustments to eradicate *Clostridium botulinum* spores.

* **[Utah State University (USU) Extension — Preserve the Harvest](https://extension.usu.edu/preserve-the-harvest/food-storage-packaging)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Utah State University Extension (Department of Nutrition, Dietetics, and Food Sciences).
  - *Application:* Academic research on long-term dry packaging: oxygen absorber capacity calculation formulas (cc ratings based on container volume and food bulk density), strict moisture safety thresholds (<10% moisture content required for anaerobic Mylar storage), and thermal degradation curves (Arrhenius equation).

* **[National Center for Home Food Preservation (NCHFP)](https://nchfp.uga.edu/)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Cooperative Extension System / USDA (University of Georgia).
  - *Application:* Scientific thermal processing tables for pressure canning, water-bath canning, pickling, and food dehydration.

* **[LDS Family Home Storage Standards & Guidelines](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/life/preparedness/long-term-food-and-water-storage?lang=eng)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Welfare and Self-Reliance Services / BYU Food Science Department.
  - *Application:* The universal 1-year dry storage baseline per adult (300–400 lbs grains, 60 lbs legumes, 60 lbs sugars, 16–24 lbs non-fat dry milk, 4 gallons cooking oil, 8–10 lbs iodized salt).

* **[FAO (UN) — Seed Processing and Long-Term Storage Guidelines](https://www.fao.org/home/en)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations.
  - *Format:* Open-access institutional manual.
  - *Application:* Protocols for building family heirloom seed vaults: drying seeds to 5–8% moisture equilibrium, germination testing methods, and vacuum/foil storage for 10+ year crop viability.

* **[U.S. Peace Corps — Small Farm Grain Storage & Wells Construction Handbooks](https://www.peacecorps.gov/)**
  - *Publishing Body:* U.S. Peace Corps / Office of Training Support (Public Domain).
  - *Application:* Low-tech appropriate technology for disaster scenarios: manual hand-dug well engineering, hand-pump maintenance, gravity sand-filter construction, and non-chemical pest protection for bulk grain silos using wood ash.

## 2. National Disaster Nutrition & Rationing Quantity Models

* **[The Sphere Handbook — Humanitarian Charter and Minimum Standards](https://emergency.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2024-05/SphereFS%2BN.pdf)**
  - *Publishing Body:* The Sphere Project (UN, Red Cross, and international NGOs).
  - *Application:* Establishes the global disaster nutrition baseline of **2,100 kcal / person / day**, macronutrient distribution (10–12% protein, 17–20% lipids), hydration minimum (3.0 L/person/day), and cold-weather climate adjustments (+100 kcal/day for each 5°C below 20°C).

* **[German Federal Ministry of Food and Agriculture (BMEL / BBK Ernährungsvorsorge)](https://www.ernaehrungsvorsorge.de/private-vorsorge/notvorrat/vorratstabelle)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Bundesministerium für Ernährung und Landwirtschaft (BMEL) and Federal Office of Civil Protection and Disaster Assistance (BBK).
  - *Application:* Official scientific 10-day emergency food stock table providing a balanced **2,200 kcal / person / day** across 7 mandatory food groups (liquids, cereals/potatoes, vegetables, fruits, dairy, meat/fish/eggs, fats/oils).

* **[Swiss Federal Office for National Economic Supply (BWL / FONES — "Notvorrat")](https://www.bwl.admin.ch/de/kluger-rat-notvorrat)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Swiss Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research (EAER / BWL).
  - *Application:* The Swiss national 7-day civil contingency standard: non-perishable foods requiring minimal cooking fuel, plus a strict minimum of 9 liters of potable water per person for immediate 3-day emergency buffer.

## 3. Civil Defense, Total Defence & Field Sanitation

* **[MSB Sweden — "If Crisis or War Comes" (Om krisen eller kriget kommer)](https://www.jonkoping.se/download/18.6a087b4c169dcff8c903056e/1556627549015/Engelska.pdf)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Swedish Civil Contingencies Agency (MSB).
  - *Application:* National resilience benchmark establishing the **7-Day Household Autonomy Standard**, unpowered heating guidelines, civilian cash buffer recommendations, and civil alert siren recognition.

* **[U.S. Army FM 21-10 / MCRP 4-11.1D — Field Hygiene and Sanitation](https://archive.org/details/FM21-10_2000)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Headquarters, Department of the Army and U.S. Marine Corps (Public Domain).
  - *Application:* Critical grid-down epidemic prevention: dry latrine and emergency pit engineering, minimum setback distances from water tables, vector control (flies/rodents), and the 3-basin field dishwashing sterilization protocol (wash, rinse, sanitize).

* **[FEMA CERT Field Operations Guide (Community Emergency Response Team)](https://quickseries.com/content/product/cert-field-operations-guide-pocket-guide)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA / Public Domain).
  - *Application:* Standardized procedures for neighborhood disaster organization, domestic utility shutoffs (water, gas, electricity), victim search marking systems, and community mutual-aid triage.

* **[Finland National Emergency Supply Agency (NESA / 72 Hours Model)](https://72tuntia.fi/)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Ministry of the Interior of Finland.
  - *Application:* Sub-zero cold-weather survival checklists and domestic home reserve standards.

## 4. Survival Fieldcraft & Military SERE Protocols

* **[U.S. Air Force AFMAN 10-644 — Survival, Evasion, Resistance, and Escape (SERE Manual)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survival,_Evasion,_Resistance_and_Escape)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Department of the Air Force (Public Domain).
  - *Application:* The military's most comprehensive survival reference: sub-zero thermal shelters (snow trenches, lean-tos with reflector fires), desert hydration conservation, the 8-step Universal Edibility Test for wilderness plants, and emergency signaling.

* **[U.S. Army Survival Manual (FM 21-76 / TC 3-21.76)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survival_manual)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Department of the Army (Public Domain).
  - *Application:* The physiological **Rule of Threes** (3 minutes oxygen, 3 hours thermal shelter, 3 days water, 3 weeks food) governing KeepReady's survival priority hierarchy.

* **[Oak Ridge National Laboratory — Nuclear War Survival Skills (NWSS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_War_Survival_Skills)**
  - *Author:* Cresson H. Kearny (Oak Ridge National Laboratory / U.S. Department of Energy - Public Domain).
  - *Application:* Empirical fallout shielding physics: the **7-10 Radiation Decay Rule**, material half-thickness shielding values, and makeshift shelter ventilation.

## 5. Austere Trauma Care & Tactical First Aid

* **[Tactical Combat Casualty Care (TCCC Guidelines / CoTCCC)](https://tccc.org.ua/files/downloads/clinical-guidelines-2026-en.pdf)**

  - *Application:* Prehospital trauma care based on the **MARCH** algorithm (Massive Hemorrhage, Airway, Respiration, Circulation, Hypothermia prevention), guiding KeepReady's trauma kit checklists.

## 6. Contingency Communications & Radio Protocols

* **[CISA Emergency Communications Ecosystem — PACE Protocol](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/leveraging-pace-plan-emergency-communications-ecosystem)**

## 7. Real-Time Hazard Feeds & Water Science

* **[openFDA Food Enforcement API](https://open.fda.gov/apis/food/enforcement/)**
  - *Publishing Body:* U.S. Food and Drug Administration.
  - *Application:* Automated cross-matching of inventory barcodes against active food recalls and biological contamination warnings.

* **[GDACS API (Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System)](https://gdacs.org/)**
  - *Publishing Body:* United Nations & European Commission.
  - *Application:* Multi-hazard real-time alerts for earthquakes, hurricanes, floods, and tsunamis without requiring API keys.

* **[NASA FIRMS API (Fire Information for Resource Management System)](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/)**
  - *Publishing Body:* NASA Earthdata / LANCE.
  - *Application:* Satellite thermal imaging (MODIS/VIIRS) detecting active wildfires around the user's home or bug-out destination.

* **[NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC) API](https://www.spaceweather.gov/)**
  - *Publishing Body:* NOAA / National Weather Service.
  - *Application:* Real-time planetary Kp-index and geomagnetic storm monitoring for electrical grid disruption early warnings.

* **[CDC Emergency Water Disinfection Protocols](https://www.cdc.gov/healthywater/emergency/making-water-safe.html)**
  - *Publishing Body:* Centers for Disease Control and Prevention / EPA.
  - *Application:* Built-in mathematical calculator converting raw collected water volume into exact chemical dosage requirements (bleach drops, chlorine dioxide, and altitude boiling times).
