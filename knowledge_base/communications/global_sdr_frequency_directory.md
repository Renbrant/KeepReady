# KeepReady — Global SDR Emergency Frequency Directory & WebSDR Presets

This directory contains verified, globally monitored High Frequency (HF / Shortwave) radio channels pre-configured as one-click bookmarks within KeepReady's integrated WebSDR and KiwiSDR monitoring engine.

## 1. Global Maritime & Humanitarian Disaster Relief Nets

These channels represent the first line of civilian and maritime emergency coordination across oceanic and continental disasters:

| Frequency (MHz) | Mode | Channel Name / Organization | Operational Scope & Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **14.300 MHz** | USB | **Maritime Mobile Service Net (MMSN)** | The world's primary civilian emergency net on 20 meters. Recognized by IARU as the Global Emergency Centre of Activity. Monitored continuously for vessels in distress, medical evacuations, and international emergency relays. |
| **14.325 MHz** | USB | **Hurricane Watch Net (HWN - Daytime)** | Activates whenever a hurricane is within 300 miles of land in the Atlantic, Caribbean, or Gulf of Mexico. Provides direct ground-truth weather reports to the National Hurricane Center (NHC / WX4NHC) in Miami. |
| **7.268 MHz** | LSB | **Waterway Net & HWN (Nighttime)** | The 40-meter band counterpart to the Hurricane Watch Net. Engaged when daytime 20m propagation closes after sunset. |
| **7.110 MHz** | LSB | **IARU Region 1 & 3 Disaster Net** | Designated emergency frequency for Europe, Africa, and Asia-Pacific. Kept clear during regional earthquakes, tsunamis, and flood relief operations. |
| **7.060 MHz** | LSB | **IARU Region 2 Emergency Net** | Designated primary emergency channel for the Americas (North and South America) on 40 meters. |
| **3.760 MHz** | LSB | **IARU 80m Nighttime Emergency Net** | Short-to-medium range regional disaster communications during darkness. |
| **21.360 MHz** | USB | **IARU 15m Global Disaster Net** | High-sunspot daytime intercontinental disaster coordination. |
