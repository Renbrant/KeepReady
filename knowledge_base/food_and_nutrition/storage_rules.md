# Knowledge Base: Food & Nutrition Storage Standards

## 1. Annual Survival Rations per Adult (Utah State University & LDS Benchmark)

Quantities calculated to sustain one healthy adult for 365 days (approx. 2,100 to 2,400 kcal/day):

* Grains (Carbohydrate Base): 135 to 180 kg (300 to 400 lbs)
  - White Polished Rice: 30 to 60 kg
  - Hard Red/White Wheat Berries: 60 to 90 kg
  - Rolled Oats: 20 to 30 kg
  - Dry Pasta / Macaroni: 20 to 30 kg
* Legumes (Plant Protein Base): 27 kg (60 lbs)
  - Dry Pinto, Black, or Navy Beans: 15 kg
  - Lentils & Split Peas: 12 kg
* Sugars & Honey (Quick Energy & Preservation): 27 kg (60 lbs)
  - Pure Honey (Indefinite shelf life) or Granulated White Sugar
* Fats & Cooking Oils (Lipid Density): 12 to 15 liters (approx. 4 gallons)
  - Coconut Oil, Olive Oil, Vegetable Oil, or Rendered Lard
* Dairy / Calcium Source: 7 to 11 kg (16 to 24 lbs)
  - Non-Fat Dry Milk (NFDM) nitrogen-packed or in Mylar with O2 absorber
* Iodized Table Salt (Electrolyte Balance): 3.6 to 4.5 kg (8 to 10 lbs)

## 2. Food Longevity Tiers (Under Ideal Storage < 21°C / 70°F)

### Tier 1: Indefinite to 30+ Years (In sealed Mylar with Oxygen Absorbers)
* White Rice (polished, non-brown)
* Whole Grain Hard Wheat Berries
* Rolled Oats
* Dry Beans and Lentils
* Dehydrated Potato Flakes (without added fats)
* Pure Honey & Granulated Sugar (Store WITHOUT oxygen absorbers)
* Table Salt & Baking Soda (Store WITHOUT oxygen absorbers)

### Tier 2: 10 to 20 Years
* Non-Fat Dry Milk (powdered)
* Freeze-Dried Fruits, Vegetables, and Meats
* Dry Pasta (egg-free durum semolina)

### Tier 3: 3 to 7 Years (Rotation Stock)
* Commercial Canned Goods (Tuna, chicken, beans, vegetables, soups)
* Multi-Vitamins and Minerals (degrades gradually)

### Tier 4: 6 to 18 Months (Rapid Oxidation / High Fat)
* Brown Rice (contains natural oils that turn rancid)
* Whole Wheat Flour (ground grains oxidize rapidly)
* Cooking Oils, Nuts, and Seeds

## 3. Oxygen Absorber Sizing & Safety Interlocks

### The Mathematical Formula:
Required O2 Capacity (cc) = (Container Volume in mL - Food Displacement Volume in mL) x 0.21

### Practical Field Ratings:
* 1-Gallon Mylar Bag (approx. 3.8 Liters): 300 cc to 500 cc
* 5-Gallon Bucket / Mylar (approx. 19 Liters) - Dense Foods (Rice, Wheat): 1,500 cc to 2,000 cc
* 5-Gallon Bucket / Mylar (approx. 19 Liters) - Aerated Foods (Pasta, Oats): 2,000 cc to 2,500 cc

### Critical Safety Alerts:
* NEVER use oxygen absorbers on sugar or salt: Moisture released by the iron oxidation reaction turns sugar and salt into solid concrete blocks.
* THE BOTULISM INTERLOCK (Moisture > 10%): Storing foods with >10% moisture content in an anaerobic (oxygen-free) sealed container creates a lethal environment for Clostridium botulinum toxin production.
