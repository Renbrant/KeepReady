# KeepReady — Comprehensive Food Commodity Catalog & Profiles

This document provides complete technical, nutritional, preservation, and preparation profiles for all major survival food commodities used in emergency bug-in storage and off-grid contingency planning.

---

## 1. Grains & Cereals

### 1.1 White Polished Rice (Long, Medium, Short Grain, Jasmine)
* Role: Primary survival carbohydrate foundation. Hypoallergenic, universally accepted, dense.
* Nutritional Profile: ~360 kcal / 100g | 7.0g Protein | 0.7g Fat | 80.0g Carbs.
* Shelf Life:
  - Retail Bag: 1 to 2 years.
  - Sealed Mylar + Oxygen Absorber: 30+ years.
  - Frozen (-18°C): Indefinite.
* Moisture Content: 11% to 13% (Safe for oxygen absorbers if properly dry).
* Cooking Water Ratio: 2:1 (200 mL water per 100g dry rice).
* Cooking Fuel & Simmer Time: Bring to boil, simmer covered 15 to 20 minutes.
* Thermal Haybox Cooking: Boil 3 minutes, trap in thermal cooker for 1 to 2 hours.
* Zero-Cook / Cold-Soak: Minute/Instant rice cold-soaks in 12 min. Raw white rice does NOT cold-soak well (remains unpalatable starch).
* Failure Modes: Grain weevil infestation, moisture clumping, rodent gnaw marks.

### 1.2 Hard Red & Hard White Wheat Berries
* Role: Complete staple grain for bread, porridge, or meat-substitute gluten. Indefinite storage benchmark.
* Nutritional Profile: ~340 kcal / 100g | 13.0g to 15.0g Protein | 2.5g Fat | 71.0g Carbs.
* Shelf Life:
  - Retail Sack: 2 to 3 years.
  - Sealed Mylar + Oxygen Absorber: 30+ years (tested viable past 35 years by BYU studies).
* Required Equipment: Manual burr grain mill (steel or stone) to produce flour, or cracked wheat for porridge.
* Cooking Water Ratio: 2.5:1 for cracked wheat porridge; 3:1 for boiled whole wheat berries.
* Simmer Time: Whole berries require 45 to 60 minutes boiling.
* Storage Interlock: Pre-freeze 72 hours before Mylar packaging to neutralize weevil eggs.

### 1.3 Rolled Oats (Flaked Oats)
* Role: High-calorie breakfast staple with soluble fiber, requiring minimal cooking water and fuel.
* Nutritional Profile: ~380 kcal / 100g | 13.5g Protein | 6.5g Fat | 68.0g Carbs.
* Shelf Life:
  - Retail Box: 12 months.
  - Sealed Mylar + Oxygen Absorber: 25 to 30 years.
* Packaging Requirement: Aerated bulk item; requires 2,000 to 2,500 cc O2 absorber per 5-gallon bucket.
* Cooking Water Ratio: 2:1 (200 mL water per 100g oats).
* Simmer Time: 3 to 5 minutes over fire.
* Zero-Cook / Cold-Soak: Fully edible and soft after 20 to 25 minutes in room-temperature water.

### 1.4 Dry Durum Semolina Pasta (Spaghetti, Macaroni)
* Role: High morale food, lightweight, easily portioned, kid-friendly.
* Nutritional Profile: ~370 kcal / 100g | 13.0g Protein | 1.5g Fat | 75.0g Carbs.
* Shelf Life:
  - Retail Box: 2 years.
  - Sealed Mylar + Oxygen Absorber: 20 to 25 years (egg-free durum pasta only).
* Puncture Warning: Sharp pasta ends easily pierce thin Mylar; use 7.0 mil Mylar or line inside of bags with parchment paper.
* Cooking Water Ratio: 3:1 (300 mL water per 100g pasta).
* Simmer Time: 9 to 12 minutes active boil. Thermal Haybox: Boil 3 min, insulate 25 min.

### 1.5 THE BROWN RICE WARNING (High-Risk Commodity)
* Risk: Brown rice retains the nutrient-rich germ and bran layers containing delicate polyunsaturated fatty acids.
* Consequence: Natural lipid oxidation occurs even inside sealed Mylar bags within 6 to 12 months, producing toxic rancidity and bitter, foul odors.
* Rule: Stock strictly **White Polished Rice** for long-term reserves.

## 2. Legumes & Pulses (Plant Protein Foundation)

### 2.1 Pinto, Black, Navy & Kidney Beans
* Role: Primary protein source when meat is unavailable. Dense in lysine, complementary amino acid profile to rice.
* Nutritional Profile: ~340 kcal / 100g | 21.0g to 24.0g Protein | 1.2g Fat | 62.0g Carbs.
* Shelf Life:
  - Retail Bag: 2 to 3 years.
  - Sealed Mylar + Oxygen Absorber: 25 to 30 years.
* The "Hard-to-Cook" Aging Phenomenon: Beans stored past 5 to 10 years develop hard seed coats that resist water penetration. Solution: Add 1/4 teaspoon of baking soda per liter of cooking water to soften aged skins.
* Cooking Water Ratio: 3.5:1 (350 mL water per 100g beans plus soaking water).
* Simmer Time: 60 to 90 minutes traditional simmer. Stovetop pressure cooker reduces this to 15 to 20 minutes (75% fuel savings).
* Raw Kidney Bean Toxicity Warning: Red kidney beans contain phytohaemagglutinin. Must be boiled vigorously for at least 10 minutes to neutralize toxins. Never slow-cook raw kidney beans below boiling.

### 2.2 Lentils (Brown, Green, Red) & Split Peas
* Role: The fastest-cooking, most fuel-efficient long-term legumes.
* Nutritional Profile: ~350 kcal / 100g | 25.0g Protein | 1.0g Fat | 60.0g Carbs.
* Shelf Life: 25+ years in sealed Mylar + O2 absorber.
* Cooking Water Ratio: 2.5:1 (250 mL water per 100g lentils).
* Simmer Time: 15 to 25 minutes (no overnight soaking required, saving half the water and time of whole beans).

## 3. Fats, Oils & Caloric Lipids

Fats provide 9 kcal/gram (more than double carbs or protein), but represent the most vulnerable shelf-life bottleneck due to lipid oxidation.

### 3.1 Pure Coconut Oil (Refined / Virgin)
* Role: The longest-lasting shelf-stable cooking fat on Earth.
* Nutritional Profile: 884 kcal / 100g | 100.0g Saturated Fat.
* Shelf Life: 2 to 4 years at room temperature in dark containers; up to 5+ years unopened in cool pantries. Highly saturated fatty acid chains resist oxidation.

### 3.2 Ghee (Clarified Butter)
* Role: Butter with all water and milk solids removed. High smoke point (250°C), dense calories.
* Shelf Life: 12 to 24 months at room temperature; up to 5 years in hermetically sealed tins.

### 3.3 Olive Oil & Vegetable Oils
* Shelf Life: 12 to 18 months max. Store in dark glass or metal tins. Once opened, consume within 60 days.

## 4. Proteins, Meats & Animal Products

### 4.1 Canned Seafood (Tuna in Oil, Sardines, Salmon)
* Role: Ready-to-eat high-value protein, omega-3 fatty acids, and salt.
* Nutritional Profile (Tuna in Oil): ~200 kcal / 100g | 29.0g Protein | 9.0g Fat | 0.0g Carbs.
* Shelf Life: 3 to 7 years past printed date if cans remain unrusted, unswollen, and undented.
* Zero-Cook: 100% ready to eat straight from the can. Conserve the oil as cooking fat or calorie booster.

### 4.2 Canned Pork / Beef / Luncheon Meat (SPAM)
* Role: Extreme caloric density, high sodium to replace electrolytes, comfort food.
* Nutritional Profile (SPAM): ~315 kcal / 100g | 13.0g Protein | 27.0g Fat | 3.0g Carbs.
* Shelf Life: 5 to 10+ years (indefinitely safe microbiologically as long as the hermetic seal holds).

### 4.3 Commercial Freeze-Dried Meats (Beef Dices, Chicken Chunks)
* Role: Longest shelf-life meat protein.
* Shelf Life: 25 to 30 years in #10 cans with oxygen absorbers.
* Rehydration: Add hot or room-temperature water for 10 to 15 minutes.

## 5. Dairy & Egg Substitutes

### 5.1 Non-Fat Dry Milk (NFDM - Powdered Desiccated Milk)
* Role: Calcium, vitamin D, complete animal protein for children and nursing mothers.
* Nutritional Profile: ~360 kcal / 100g | 36.0g Protein | 0.8g Fat | 52.0g Carbs.
* Shelf Life: 20+ years in Mylar with O2 absorber.
* Storage Rule: Stock strictly Non-Fat dry milk. Whole powdered milk contains milk fat that rancidifies in 12 to 18 months.

### 5.2 Whole Powdered Eggs
* Shelf Life: 5 to 10 years in sealed #10 nitrogen-packed cans.
* Utility: Scrambled eggs, baking binder for bread, caloric breakfast.

## 6. Sweeteners, Leaveners & Baking Essentials

### 6.1 Pure Natural Honey
* Role: Universal antibiotic wound dressing, sore throat treatment, and non-perishable glucose energy.
* Shelf Life: Indefinite (found edible in 3,000-year-old Egyptian tombs).
* Natural Crystallization: Honey crystallizes over time; simply place container in warm water (<45°C) to reliquefy.

### 6.2 Granulated White Sugar
* Shelf Life: Indefinite.
* CRITICAL STORAGE RULE: Never use an oxygen absorber. Store in airtight buckets with silica gel desiccants only.

### 6.3 Baking Soda (Sodium Bicarbonate)
* Role: Bread leavening, tooth cleaning, fire extinguishing, acid neutralization, bean softening.
* Shelf Life: Indefinite if kept sealed from humidity.

## 7. Spices, Salts & Appetite Fatigue Defense

* Iodized Salt: Biological necessity, electrolyte regulator. Indefinite shelf life. Store dry without O2 absorbers.
* Whole Black Peppercorns: Store whole in Mylar. Grind fresh with manual grinder (whole corns last 20+ years; ground pepper loses aroma in 1 year).
* Chicken / Beef Bouillon Cubes: High-salt soup bases. Lifespan 2 to 4 years.
* Commercial White Vinegar (5% Acidity): Indefinite shelf life. Food pickling, surface sanitizing, water scale removal.
