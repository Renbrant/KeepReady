# Knowledge Base: Tactical Medicine & Trauma Care (TCCC MARCH)

## 1. The MARCH Resuscitation Algorithm (DoD CoTCCC Standard)

In catastrophic emergencies where emergency medical services are unavailable, trauma care follows the strict MARCH hierarchy:

* M — Massive Hemorrhage (Priority 1):
  - Action: Immediately apply a windlass combat tourniquet (CAT or SOF-T) high and tight on the limb. Pack junctional wounds with hemostatic gauze (Kaolin/Chitosan) and apply direct pressure for 3 minutes.
* A — Airway (Priority 2):
  - Action: Inspect airway, clear obstructions, place unconscious breathing casualties in the recovery position or insert a nasopharyngeal airway (NPA).
* R — Respiration (Priority 3):
  - Action: Seal all penetrating chest wounds with an occlusive vented chest seal. Monitor for tension pneumothorax.
* C — Circulation (Priority 4):
  - Action: Reassess tourniquets for bleeding cessation. Check peripheral pulse, capillary refill, and treat for hypovolemic shock.
* H — Hypothermia Prevention (Priority 5):
  - Action: Wrap the patient in an insulated hypothermia survival blanket. Trauma patients lose thermal regulation rapidly, triggering deadly coagulopathy even in warm climates.

## 2. Trauma Kit Supply Manifest (IFAK / Blowout Kit)

Essential non-perishable medical components:
* 2x Combat Application Tourniquets (CAT Gen 7)
* 2x Hemostatic Gauze Packs (QuikClot or ChitoGauze)
* 2x Vented Chest Seals (Foxseal / HyFin)
* 1x 4-Inch or 6-Inch Emergency Pressure Bandage (Israeli Bandage)
* 1x Nasopharyngeal Airway (28 Fr with lubricating jelly)
* 2x Heavy-Duty Mylar Hypothermia Blankets
* 1x Trauma Shears (7.5 inch stainless steel)
