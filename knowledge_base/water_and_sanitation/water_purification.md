# Knowledge Base: Water Purification & Field Sanitation

## 1. Human Water Consumption Standards

* Survival Minimum (Drinking only): 2.0 to 2.5 Liters / person / day.
* Sphere Project Baseline (Drinking, rehydration, basic cooking): 3.0 Liters / person / day.
* Total Civil Contingency (Drinking + basic hygiene): 4.0 to 6.0 Liters / person / day.

---

## 2. Chemical Water Disinfection Protocols (CDC / EPA Guidelines)

When boiling is not possible, use common unscented liquid household bleach (Sodium Hypochlorite 6% to 8.25%):

* Clear Water:
  - 1 Liter: 2 drops of bleach.
  - 1 Gallon (approx. 3.8 L): 8 drops of bleach.
  - Contact Time: Mix thoroughly and let stand for 30 minutes. Water should have a slight chlorine odor.
* Cloudy / Turbid Water:
  - Pre-filter water through clean cloth or paper filter first.
  - 1 Liter: 4 drops of bleach.
  - 1 Gallon: 16 drops of bleach.
  - Contact Time: Let stand for 60 minutes.

### Thermal Boiling Protocol:
* Sea Level to 1,000 meters (3,300 ft): Bring water to a rolling boil for 1 full minute.
* Above 1,000 meters (> 3,300 ft): Boil vigorously for 3 full minutes (lower atmospheric pressure reduces boiling point).

## 3. Field Sanitation & Waste Engineering (U.S. Army FM 21-10)

In grid-down emergencies, waterborne enteric diseases (cholera, dysentery) represent the leading cause of mortality.

* The 30-Meter Water Setback Rule: All field latrines, cat-holes, and waste pits must be located at least 30 meters (100 feet) down-slope from any natural water source, well, or rain catchment barrel.
* The 3-Basin Dish Sterilization Method:
  1. Basin 1: Warm wash water with biodegradable soap.
  2. Basin 2: Clean rinse water.
  3. Basin 3: Sanitizing soak with 1 capful of household bleach per gallon of water (minimum 1 minute contact time).
